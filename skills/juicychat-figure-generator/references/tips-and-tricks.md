# JuicyChat image tips (starter)

Public wiki + common community practice. Keep prompts short.

## Official starting points

- Image process: wiki.juicychat.ai/creation-guide/iv.image-generation-process
- Adult / ban rules: wiki.juicychat.ai/art-generation-guidelines/images-and-media

## Length

If the field rejects the prompt, cut in this order: extra quality tags → setting nouns → synonyms. Keep the adult lock and the identity tags.

A working prompt is often 200–450 characters.

## Figure vs scene

| Job | Put in the prompt |
|---|---|
| New Figure | adult lock + hair/eyes/skin/body + style |
| PFP / cover | add clothes, pose, place |
| Remix | only the change |

Clothes baked into a Figure will fight every Remix. Prefer a clean Figure.

## Duo

Lead with `2women, duo`. Keep the rest short. If the second body vanishes, stop and make two solos.

## Review ratios (wiki)

Head-to-shoulder and head-to-body both need to look adult. `adult_proportions, adult_face` plus `full_body` is the practical fix.

## Photorealism

The wiki bans photorealism as a style. Stay on `anime_style` / `illustration`.
