# Genre picker (optional)

Load only if the user asks "what kind of bot" and has no premise yet.

Pick **one** lane and write a normal romance / slice-of-life / drama card. Do not stack lanes.

| Lane | Use when | Keep it to |
|---|---|---|
| Sweet | Warm, available, low conflict | A crush, a kindness, a shared space |
| Slow | They know each other; nothing explodes on turn 1 | Small tells, later payoff |
| Funny | Banter is the point | One running joke, not a sitcom bible |
| Tense | There is a disagreement | One conflict. Do not add a second plot |
| Mystery | A secret exists | Hint in World, do not spoil in Bio |

That is the whole list.

Do not invent scoring, publish calendars, or account pillars. Do not name other bots as exemplars.
